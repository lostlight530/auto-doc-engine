## 1. Rationale

Stage A established that typed lineage, multi-format derivation, versioned correction, and target-specific validation were already converging in Q1 2024

Stage B asks what happened next as computational-document systems became more stateful and more explicit about workspace state, conversion semantics, renderer/compiler dependencies, citation correctness, and path/security boundaries

This is external frontier reconstruction, not repository history

## 2. Repository lens and non-claims

Lens: research artifacts, document evidence, provenance, process disclosure, typed lineage, research objects, artifact durability, and multi-format research records

- workspace export/import != computational reproduction

## 3. Research questions

1. **RQ1:** How did Q2 2024 computational-document environments make workspace and document state more explicit, and what state remained outside the rendered surface?
2. **RQ2:** How did converter/compiler releases expose representation-specific structure, target semantics, and forward-correction requirements?
3. **RQ3:** What did Q2 security, citation, caption, and template fixes reveal about artifact provenance beyond a final-file hash?

## 4. Temporal and conceptual scope

Included: JupyterLab/Notebook workspace and rendering state, Pandoc conversion/version semantics, Typst compiler/citation/security fixes, Q2 release/patch evidence

Excluded: broad notebook adoption, publisher acceptance, independent scientific reproduction, all document systems, and current repository implementation claims

## 5. Source authority plan
