# July 2025 Reconstruction — CodeMeta 3.0

## Monthly event
CodeMeta 3.0 was released on 2025-07-13.

The release expands the vocabulary/context for describing research software through explicit relationships among source code, software applications, contributor roles, and review records, while also carrying a v2-to-v3 crosswalk.

## Historical interpretation
The July story is one of **metadata becoming less flat**.

A software record can increasingly distinguish:
- the source representation from the application it realizes;
- the person from a time-bounded role;
- the existence of a review record from the validity or authority of that review.

```text
metadata richness
!= factual verification

typed relation
!= proved relation
```

## Month boundary
No CodeMeta validation or migration was executed locally. July contributes a vocabulary/version event to the historical narrative, not a repository capability change.
